Momentum After the Rate Peak
----------------------------

Momentum has historically weakened in the six months after a policy rate peak.
We test whether that holds across regions and what it means for a factor book
today.

Hermes Research publishing as of 6 November 2026


Key Points
----------

- Momentum's drawdown risk doubles after a rate peak, in every region tested.
- Quality offsets most of it at a fraction of the turnover.
- We cut the momentum tilt by a third and add to quality.
