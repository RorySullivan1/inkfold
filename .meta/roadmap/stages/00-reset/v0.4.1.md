  variables and every reference and repository link, as v0.4.0 did, keeping history as it was.

- The wheel's only top-level package is `inkfold`, and `import inkfold` renders an email from it
  in a clean venv.
- Neither former name remains outside history, enforced by `tests/test_distribution.py`.
- Every golden is byte-identical unless a name is rendered.
- The full suite, ruff, ruff format and mypy pass; the prose baseline does not grow.
